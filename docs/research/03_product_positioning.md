# 03 Product Positioning

## Who We Are

ux-evaluation-tool is a design-image-level human factors and UX risk evaluation tool.

It is a local H5 tool for product, design, user research, and human factors teams. Users upload UI or product design images, enter device and scenario parameters, choose target users and rule sets, then receive explainable on-image risk annotations.

## What We Are Not

- Not a generic AI UI review tool.
- Not a pure WCAG checker.
- Not a Maze or UserTesting-style usability testing platform.
- Not a first-stage Figma plugin.
- Not a full enterprise compliance platform.
- Not a real eye-tracking prediction product in the first stage.

## Why We Exist

Teams often need to review design images before implementation or formal usability testing. Existing accessibility tools, attention heatmap tools, design system tools, and professional ergonomics platforms each solve part of the problem, but ordinary teams lack a lightweight way to connect human factors, UX risk, and rule explanations directly on design images.

## Core Users

- Product managers evaluating early UI or product screen proposals.
- Product designers and interaction designers checking operational risk.
- User researchers preparing usability risk reviews.
- Human factors specialists communicating findings to product teams.
- HMI, IoT, appliance, and device-screen teams working before implementation.

## First-Stage Scenarios

- Mobile UI.
- Automotive center display UI.
- Appliance and IoT screen UI.

## Differentiation

The core differentiation is human factors + UX + explainable rule layer.

The tool evaluates design-image risks using accessibility, platform guidance, human factors theory, domain rules, and custom rules. It should explain whether a finding is a normative rule match, theory-based inference, heuristic risk, or custom rule finding.

## Not Prioritized in Stage One

- Real eye-tracking prediction.
- Full AI automatic recognition.
- Enterprise collaboration platform.
- Complete multi-country compliance system.
- Figma pluginization.
